# 1. Scene
A 1920s Korean village meeting room at night seen as a hand-painted watercolor memory: a warm ondol room with paper doors, a young teacher in a cotton durumagi pointing at large Korean letter cards pinned on the wall, village children and a few grandmothers seated on the floor with slates, an oil lamp, bolts of plain cotton cloth stacked by the door. In the center foreground a Korean girl about ten years old in a plain cotton jeogori and dark skirt with a single braid holds up a slate with a single large chalk circle on it, beaming. The lower third of the canvas stays quiet and uncluttered so a dialogue panel can sit there, and the top sixth stays plain. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting across the whole album.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level medium-wide view, 35mm-like field, the main figures at mid distance slightly left of center, background softens into pale wash.
# 3. Lighting
Warm oil-lamp light from the lower left, deep soft shadows in the corners, gentle glow on the faces.
# 4. Color grading
Sepia watercolor palette: paper cream #EFE4CF, sepia wash #C9A574, deep ink brown #3B2A1E, muted indigo #5B6E8C, faded rust #9A4A3B. Around 3800K, low saturation.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish.

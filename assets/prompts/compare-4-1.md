# 1. Scene
A single image divided into two equal side-by-side panels by a thin plain cream gutter, both panels painted in the same hand as a hand-painted watercolor memory. Both panels show the same composition: a 1945 Korean classroom with rows of low desks, children in uniforms seated, a teacher at a blackboard, a window, a wall clock face with plain tick marks. The right panel repeats the left panel exactly except for three deliberate differences: in the right panel the window shows a faint pale blue sky with soft color, the teacher holds a bundle of papers instead of a pointer, and the children are standing up with raised hands. Every other element stays identical in both panels. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting across the whole album.

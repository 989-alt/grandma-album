# 1. Scene
A 1941 Korean farmhouse yard seen as a hand-painted watercolor memory: a pile of brass bowls, pots and spoons heaped on a straw mat, a clerk with a ledger and a cart waiting at the gate, the mother carrying a brass bowl toward the pile with a tight face. In the center foreground a Korean girl about eleven years old in a plain cotton jeogori and dark skirt with braided hair kneels by the kitchen door with a single pair of brass spoon and chopsticks half hidden in her sleeve. The lower third of the canvas stays quiet and uncluttered so a dialogue panel can sit there, and the top sixth stays plain. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting across the whole album.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level medium-wide view, 35mm-like field, the main figures at mid distance slightly left of center, background softens into pale wash.
# 3. Lighting
Low evening light from the left, long warm shadows, cool shade on the far side.
# 4. Color grading
Sepia watercolor palette: paper cream #EFE4CF, sepia wash #C9A574, deep ink brown #3B2A1E, muted indigo #5B6E8C, faded rust #9A4A3B. Around 3800K, low saturation.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish.

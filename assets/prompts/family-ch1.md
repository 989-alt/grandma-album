# 1. Scene
A sprite sheet of two oval miniature sepia bust portraits in one row with even spacing on a flat solid magenta background, each inside a thin brass oval frame, an 1880s Korean couple: a father in his thirties with a thin beard wearing a white jeogori and a black horsehair hat, and a mother in her thirties with hair in a low bun wearing a white jeogori. Calm gentle faces painted in sepia tones only. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms held inside a crisp ink outline so every silhouette cuts cleanly, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting as the approved album concept.
# 2. Camera
Straight-on orthographic view, the two ovals the same size and vertically aligned.
# 3. Lighting
Soft warm lamplight from the upper left, faint contact shadow under each piece.
# 4. Color grading
Album palette: paper cream #EFE4CF, sepia wash #C9A574, ink brown #3B2A1E, indigo #5B6E8C on flat magenta #FF00FF. Around 3800K, low saturation.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor with ink outline, fully opaque paint, crisp silhouettes against the flat magenta, blank surfaces stay blank.

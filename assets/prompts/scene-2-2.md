# 1. Scene
A 1907 Korean mountain path seen as a hand-painted watercolor memory: pine trees and rocky slope, a small band of young righteous-army volunteers in worn white hanbok and a few old military jackets resting by a stream, carrying old rifles and bamboo spears, a fire of twigs. In the center foreground a Korean girl about ten years old in a plain cotton jeogori and dark skirt with a single braid offers a cloth bundle of rice balls to a tired young soldier who kneels to take it. The lower third of the canvas stays quiet and uncluttered so a dialogue panel can sit there, and the top sixth stays plain. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting across the whole album.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level medium-wide view, 35mm-like field, the main figures at mid distance slightly left of center, background softens into pale wash.
# 3. Lighting
Low evening light from the left, long warm shadows, cool shade on the far side.
# 4. Color grading
Sepia watercolor palette: paper cream #EFE4CF, sepia wash #C9A574, deep ink brown #3B2A1E, muted indigo #5B6E8C, faded rust #9A4A3B. Around 3800K, low saturation.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish.

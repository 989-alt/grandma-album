# 1. Scene
December 1950 Hungnam harbor seen as a hand-painted watercolor memory with soft color: a gray winter sea, a huge dark cargo ship crowded with people on every deck, a dense crowd on the pier pushing toward the gangway, snow in the air, bundles and dropped shoes on the ground. In the center foreground a Korean girl about eleven years old in a plain cotton jeogori and dark skirt with braided hair reaches back through the crowd with an outstretched hand toward her father whose hat is just visible being carried away by the crowd. The lower third of the canvas stays quiet and uncluttered so a dialogue panel can sit there, and the top sixth stays plain. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting across the whole album.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level medium-wide view, 35mm-like field, the main figures at mid distance slightly left of center, background softens into pale wash.
# 3. Lighting
Flat cold winter light with snow in the air, muted gray shadows, a faint warm glow from a lantern on the pier.
# 4. Color grading
Sepia watercolor base with the first gentle natural color seeping in: paper cream #EFE4CF, sepia wash #C9A574, ink brown #3B2A1E, soft teal #6FA3A8 and faded rust #9A4A3B as the only tinted accents. Around 4200K, low saturation.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish.

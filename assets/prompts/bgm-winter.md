Somber winter journey theme for a children's history story game, a family walking through snow during wartime evacuation. Low cello and viola in slow unison, a distant soft wind texture, a single plaintive haegeum line, 60 BPM, minor key, restrained and dignified, sorrowful without being frightening for ten-year-olds. Seamlessly loopable, about 60 seconds. Instrumental only, no vocals.

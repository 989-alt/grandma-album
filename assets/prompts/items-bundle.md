# 1. Scene
A sprite sheet of six painted game item cards in two rows of three with generous even spacing, each item centered in its cell, on a flat solid magenta background. Row one: a brass spoon beside a pair of brass chopsticks, a square cloth bundle tied at the top, an old thread-bound book with a worn plain indigo cover and a blank cream label strip. Row two: a woven basket of white radishes with green tops, a small faded sepia photograph of a family in a paper corner mount, a small brown medicine bottle with a plain cork and a blank paper label. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms held inside a crisp ink outline so every silhouette cuts cleanly, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting as the approved album concept.
# 2. Camera
Straight-on orthographic view, every item the same visual weight and size, aligned to the grid.
# 3. Lighting
Soft warm lamplight from the upper left, faint contact shadow under each piece.
# 4. Color grading
Album palette: paper cream #EFE4CF, sepia wash #C9A574, ink brown #3B2A1E, indigo #5B6E8C on flat magenta #FF00FF. Around 3800K, low saturation.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor with ink outline, fully opaque paint, crisp silhouettes against the flat magenta, blank surfaces stay blank.

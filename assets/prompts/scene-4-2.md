# 1. Scene
A late 1945 Korean wooden veranda seen as a hand-painted watercolor memory with soft color: a stack of fresh newspapers and thin children's magazines on the floorboards, a young man in a worn coat just home with a travel bag setting down a parcel, the mother wiping her eyes. In the center foreground a Korean girl about eleven years old in a plain cotton jeogori and dark skirt with braided hair sits cross-legged with a magazine open on her lap, reading aloud to her little brother. The lower third of the canvas stays quiet and uncluttered so a dialogue panel can sit there, and the top sixth stays plain. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting across the whole album.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level medium-wide view, 35mm-like field, the main figures at mid distance slightly left of center, background softens into pale wash.
# 3. Lighting
Clear morning sunlight from the upper right, long soft shadows, bright but gentle.
# 4. Color grading
Sepia watercolor base with the first gentle natural color seeping in: paper cream #EFE4CF, sepia wash #C9A574, ink brown #3B2A1E, soft teal #6FA3A8 and faded rust #9A4A3B as the only tinted accents. Around 4200K, low saturation.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish.

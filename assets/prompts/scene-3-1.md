# 1. Scene
A 1938 Korean railway station yard seen as a hand-painted watercolor memory: a black steam locomotive hissing at a platform, freight cars being loaded with mountains of rice sacks by porters, soldiers in khaki standing by, hand carts lined up on the dirt. In the center foreground a Korean girl about eleven years old in a plain cotton jeogori and dark skirt with braided hair counts carts on her fingers beside her little brother who stares at the locomotive. The lower third of the canvas stays quiet and uncluttered so a dialogue panel can sit there, and the top sixth stays plain. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting across the whole album.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level medium-wide view, 35mm-like field, the main figures at mid distance slightly left of center, background softens into pale wash.
# 3. Lighting
Overcast daylight from the upper left, soft open shadows with gentle contrast.
# 4. Color grading
Sepia watercolor palette: paper cream #EFE4CF, sepia wash #C9A574, deep ink brown #3B2A1E, muted indigo #5B6E8C, faded rust #9A4A3B. Around 3800K, low saturation.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish.

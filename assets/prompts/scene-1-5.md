# 1. Scene
An 1890s Korean shop front of a foreign goods store seen as a hand-painted watercolor memory: a glass window displaying a bicycle and a brass horn gramophone under a plain blank wooden signboard, a stack of folded newspapers on a stool by the door, a tin cup of dark coffee on a small table. In the center foreground a Korean boy about ten years old in a white cotton jeogori and baggy trousers with a long braid down his back holds an unfolded newspaper up to an elderly man in white who leans in to listen, the page a plain soft gray wash. Every signboard, banner, wall, blackboard and page in the picture is a plain blank surface. The lower third of the canvas stays quiet and uncluttered so a dialogue panel can sit there, and the top sixth stays plain. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting across the whole album.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level medium-wide view, 35mm-like field, the main figures at mid distance slightly left of center, background softens into pale wash.
# 3. Lighting
Low evening light from the left, long warm shadows, cool shade on the far side.
# 4. Color grading
Sepia watercolor palette: paper cream #EFE4CF, sepia wash #C9A574, deep ink brown #3B2A1E, muted indigo #5B6E8C, faded rust #9A4A3B. Around 3800K, low saturation.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish.

# 1. Scene
A 1942 Korean room at night seen as a hand-painted watercolor memory: an oil lamp on a low table, the father reading a creased letter with a small folded map, the mother holding the lamp close, paper doors shut, a wall cabinet with a loose board. In the center foreground a Korean girl about eleven years old in a plain cotton jeogori and dark skirt with braided hair leans in over the table, eyes on the letter, while her little brother sleeps under a quilt. The lower third of the canvas stays quiet and uncluttered so a dialogue panel can sit there, and the top sixth stays plain. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting across the whole album.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level medium-wide view, 35mm-like field, the main figures at mid distance slightly left of center, background softens into pale wash.
# 3. Lighting
Warm oil-lamp light from the lower left, deep soft shadows in the corners, gentle glow on the faces.
# 4. Color grading
Sepia watercolor palette: paper cream #EFE4CF, sepia wash #C9A574, deep ink brown #3B2A1E, muted indigo #5B6E8C, faded rust #9A4A3B. Around 3800K, low saturation.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish.

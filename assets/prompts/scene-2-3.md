# 1. Scene
A 1911 Korean common school classroom seen as a hand-painted watercolor memory: rows of low wooden desks with children in dark uniforms sitting stiffly, a teacher in a dark uniform with a sword at his belt standing before a blank blackboard holding a pointer, a bare lightbulb, bare plaster walls. In the center foreground a Korean girl about ten years old in a plain cotton jeogori and dark skirt with a single braid sits at a front desk with her hands folded on a closed textbook, eyes lowered. Every signboard, banner, wall, blackboard and page in the picture is a plain blank surface. The lower third of the canvas stays quiet and uncluttered so a dialogue panel can sit there, and the top sixth stays plain. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting across the whole album.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level medium-wide view, 35mm-like field, the main figures at mid distance slightly left of center, background softens into pale wash.
# 3. Lighting
Overcast daylight from the upper left, soft open shadows with gentle contrast.
# 4. Color grading
Sepia watercolor palette: paper cream #EFE4CF, sepia wash #C9A574, deep ink brown #3B2A1E, muted indigo #5B6E8C, faded rust #9A4A3B. Around 3800K, low saturation.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish.

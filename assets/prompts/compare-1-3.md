# 1. Scene
A single image divided into two equal side-by-side panels by a thin plain cream gutter, both panels painted in the same hand as a hand-painted watercolor memory. Both panels show the same composition: an 1880s Korean town street with tile-roofed shops whose signboards and hanging cloth banners are plain blank surfaces, a stone wall, villagers in white hanbok and horsehair hats, an ox cart, a bare tree. The right panel repeats the left panel exactly except for three deliberate differences: in the right panel one villager wears a dark western suit and bowler hat, a two-story western brick building with glass windows replaces the far shop, and a tall wooden pole with a single wire stands by the road. Every other element stays identical in both panels. Every signboard and banner in both panels stays a plain blank surface. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting across the whole album.

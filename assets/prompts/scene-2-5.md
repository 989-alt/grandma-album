# 1. Scene
A March 1919 Seoul back alley seen as a hand-painted watercolor memory: narrow lane between tile-roofed houses, neighbors leaning out of gates whispering, a distant crowd with raised bare arms and white sleeves glimpsed at the lane's end, a dropped straw shoe. In the center foreground a Korean girl about ten years old in a plain cotton jeogori and dark skirt with a single braid crouches by a stone wall, pressing a folded white cloth into a crack between the stones while her mother keeps watch at the gate. Every person in the picture holds nothing in their hands except the girl. The lower third of the canvas stays quiet and uncluttered so a dialogue panel can sit there, and the top sixth stays plain. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting across the whole album.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level medium-wide view, 35mm-like field, the main figures at mid distance slightly left of center, background softens into pale wash.
# 3. Lighting
Overcast daylight from the upper left, soft open shadows with gentle contrast.
# 4. Color grading
Sepia watercolor palette: paper cream #EFE4CF, sepia wash #C9A574, deep ink brown #3B2A1E, muted indigo #5B6E8C, faded rust #9A4A3B. Around 3800K, low saturation.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish.

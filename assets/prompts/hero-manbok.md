# 1. Scene
A sprite sheet of three bust portraits of the same character in one row with even spacing on a flat solid magenta background: a Korean boy about ten years old in a white cotton jeogori and baggy trousers with a long braid down his back, an 1880s Korean boy with a round face and bright eyes. Left: calm neutral expression. Middle: wide happy grin. Right: worried, brows drawn together and lips pressed. Same clothes, same hair, same size in all three. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms held inside a crisp ink outline so every silhouette cuts cleanly, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting as the approved album concept.
# 2. Camera
Straight-on orthographic view, every piece the same visual weight and size, aligned to the row.
# 3. Lighting
Soft warm lamplight from the upper left, faint contact shadow under each piece.
# 4. Color grading
Album palette: paper cream #EFE4CF, sepia wash #C9A574, ink brown #3B2A1E, indigo #5B6E8C on flat magenta #FF00FF. Around 3800K, low saturation.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor with ink outline, fully opaque paint, crisp silhouettes against the flat magenta, blank surfaces stay blank.

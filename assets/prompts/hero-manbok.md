# 1. Scene
A sprite sheet of three bust portraits of the same character in one row with even spacing on a flat solid magenta background: an 1880s Korean little boy about ten years old, clearly a boy: sturdy square jaw, thick straight brows, short fringe over the forehead with the rest of his hair tied in a single braid hanging behind his back, wearing a white cotton jeogori with a plain cloth tie and baggy trousers. Left: calm neutral expression. Middle: wide happy grin. Right: worried, brows drawn together and lips pressed. Same clothes, same hair, same size in all three. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms held inside a crisp ink outline so every silhouette cuts cleanly, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting as the approved album concept.

Straight-on orthographic view, every piece the same visual weight and size, aligned to the row.
# 3. Lighting

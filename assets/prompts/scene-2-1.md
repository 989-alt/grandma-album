# 1. Scene
A 1905 Korean shopping street seen as a hand-painted watercolor memory: a row of small wooden shops with their shutters closed and doors barred, a few merchants standing outside with folded arms, a group of students in dark school uniforms walking away from a school gate. In the center foreground a Korean girl about ten years old in a plain cotton jeogori and dark skirt with a single braid stands before her father's closed shop, hand on the shutter, looking up at him as he stares down the empty street. The lower third of the canvas stays quiet and uncluttered so a dialogue panel can sit there, and the top sixth stays plain. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting across the whole album.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level medium-wide view, 35mm-like field, the main figures at mid distance slightly left of center, background softens into pale wash.
# 3. Lighting
Overcast daylight from the upper left, soft open shadows with gentle contrast.
# 4. Color grading
Sepia watercolor palette: paper cream #EFE4CF, sepia wash #C9A574, deep ink brown #3B2A1E, muted indigo #5B6E8C, faded rust #9A4A3B. Around 3800K, low saturation.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish.

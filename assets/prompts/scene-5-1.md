# 1. Scene
A June 1950 Korean farmhouse yard at dawn seen as a hand-painted watercolor memory with soft color: pale gray-blue dawn light, the family hurriedly packing cloth bundles on the ground, a rolled quilt, a basket of radishes, a stack of books, a small photograph, a medicine bottle, the father tying a bundle. In the center foreground a Korean girl about eleven years old in a plain cotton jeogori and dark skirt with braided hair kneels over an open square cloth deciding what to put in. The lower third of the canvas stays quiet and uncluttered so a dialogue panel can sit there, and the top sixth stays plain. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting across the whole album.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level medium-wide view, 35mm-like field, the main figures at mid distance slightly left of center, background softens into pale wash.
# 3. Lighting
Pale gray-blue dawn light from the right, cool soft shadows, a faint warm glow from the kitchen door.
# 4. Color grading
Sepia watercolor base with the first gentle natural color seeping in: paper cream #EFE4CF, sepia wash #C9A574, ink brown #3B2A1E, soft teal #6FA3A8 and faded rust #9A4A3B as the only tinted accents. Around 4200K, low saturation.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish.

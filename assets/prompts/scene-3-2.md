# 1. Scene
A 1938 Seoul street seen as a hand-painted watercolor memory: a boy on a bicycle balancing a tall stack of noodle bowls on one shoulder, racing past a row of low tile-roofed Korean shops with plain blank cloth signs toward a distant modern district with a tall department store with a plain smooth stone facade and electric poles. In the center foreground a Korean girl about eleven years old in a plain cotton jeogori and dark skirt with braided hair and her little brother stand on the roadside watching the delivery boy pass, mouths open. Every signboard, banner, wall, blackboard and page in the picture is a plain blank surface. The lower third of the canvas stays quiet and uncluttered so a dialogue panel can sit there, and the top sixth stays plain. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting across the whole album.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level medium-wide view, 35mm-like field, the main figures at mid distance slightly left of center, background softens into pale wash.
# 3. Lighting
Clear morning sunlight from the upper right, long soft shadows, bright but gentle.
# 4. Color grading
Sepia watercolor palette: paper cream #EFE4CF, sepia wash #C9A574, deep ink brown #3B2A1E, muted indigo #5B6E8C, faded rust #9A4A3B. Around 3800K, low saturation.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish.

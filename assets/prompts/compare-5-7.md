# 1. Scene
A single image divided into two equal side-by-side panels by a thin plain cream gutter, both panels painted in the same hand as a hand-painted watercolor memory. Both panels show the same composition: a 1946 Korean country road crossing low hills with a single wooden barrier post, a guard hut, farmers walking across with bundles, a tree. The right panel repeats the left panel exactly except for three deliberate differences: in the right panel a tall barbed wire fence with concrete posts runs across the whole road, the farmers stand still on the near side, and the guard hut has become a watchtower. Every other element stays identical in both panels. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting across the whole album.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level medium-wide view, identical framing in both panels, background softens into pale wash.
# 3. Lighting
Overcast daylight from the upper left, soft open shadows with gentle contrast.

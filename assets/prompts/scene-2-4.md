# 1. Scene
A 1912 Korean rice paddy under survey seen as a hand-painted watercolor memory: terraced paddies, two officials in dark uniforms with a surveying tripod and a measuring chain, a farmer in white standing aside with his hat in his hands, a young man with a travel bundle on his shoulder walking away along the ridge toward distant mountains. In the center foreground a Korean girl about ten years old in a plain cotton jeogori and dark skirt with a single braid waves after the young man from the field edge. The lower third of the canvas stays quiet and uncluttered so a dialogue panel can sit there, and the top sixth stays plain. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting across the whole album.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level medium-wide view, 35mm-like field, the main figures at mid distance slightly left of center, background softens into pale wash.
# 3. Lighting
Clear morning sunlight from the upper right, long soft shadows, bright but gentle.
# 4. Color grading
Sepia watercolor palette: paper cream #EFE4CF, sepia wash #C9A574, deep ink brown #3B2A1E, muted indigo #5B6E8C, faded rust #9A4A3B. Around 3800K, low saturation.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish.

# 1. Scene
A single image divided into two equal side-by-side panels by a thin plain cream gutter, both panels painted in the same hand as a hand-painted watercolor memory. Both panels show the same composition: a 1938 Korean neighborhood lane with low tile-roofed houses, a stone wall, a woman carrying a water jar, a child with a hoop, a tree. The right panel repeats the left panel exactly except for three deliberate differences: in the right panel the far house becomes a tall modern department store with lit windows and a plain smooth stone facade, an electric pole with lamp stands at the corner, and the water jar becomes a tap on a brick post. Every other element stays identical in both panels. Every wall, sign and window in both panels is plain and blank. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting across the whole album.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level medium-wide view, identical framing in both panels, background softens into pale wash.
# 3. Lighting
Overcast daylight from the upper left, soft open shadows with gentle contrast.

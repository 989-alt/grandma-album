# 1. Scene
A 1953 Korean wooden veranda seen as a hand-painted watercolor memory with soft color: a low dining table set with bowls and a single pair of brass spoon and chopsticks laid at one place, a family seated around it in their best hanbok posed for a photograph, an empty wooden picture frame leaning against the wall at the end of the table. In the center foreground a Korean girl about eleven years old in a plain cotton jeogori and dark skirt with braided hair as a teenager sits at the table with a small, steady smile, one hand resting beside the brass spoon. The lower third of the canvas stays quiet and uncluttered so a dialogue panel can sit there, and the top sixth stays plain. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting across the whole album.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level medium-wide view, 35mm-like field, the main figures at mid distance slightly left of center, background softens into pale wash.
# 3. Lighting
Low evening light from the left, long warm shadows, cool shade on the far side.
# 4. Color grading
Sepia watercolor base with the first gentle natural color seeping in: paper cream #EFE4CF, sepia wash #C9A574, ink brown #3B2A1E, soft teal #6FA3A8 and faded rust #9A4A3B as the only tinted accents. Around 4200K, low saturation.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish.

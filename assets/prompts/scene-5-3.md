# 1. Scene
A 1951 refugee tent school seen as a hand-painted watercolor memory with soft color: a large canvas tent open at the side, children seated on the ground with slates, a teacher pointing at a blank board propped on a crate, a small stall with a plain blank cloth sign nearby serving bowls of thick wheat noodles. In the center foreground a Korean girl about eleven years old in a plain cotton jeogori and dark skirt with braided hair hands a worn book to a friend beside her, both smiling. Every signboard, banner, wall, blackboard and page in the picture is a plain blank surface. The lower third of the canvas stays quiet and uncluttered so a dialogue panel can sit there, and the top sixth stays plain. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting across the whole album.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level medium-wide view, 35mm-like field, the main figures at mid distance slightly left of center, background softens into pale wash.
# 3. Lighting
Clear morning sunlight from the upper right, long soft shadows, bright but gentle.
# 4. Color grading
Sepia watercolor base with the first gentle natural color seeping in: paper cream #EFE4CF, sepia wash #C9A574, ink brown #3B2A1E, soft teal #6FA3A8 and faded rust #9A4A3B as the only tinted accents. Around 4200K, low saturation.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish.

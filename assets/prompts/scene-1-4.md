# 1. Scene
An 1899 Seoul street with the first electric tram seen as a hand-painted watercolor memory: an open wooden streetcar with a trolley pole on overhead wire stopped at a stop, passengers in white hanbok and a few in dark western suits climbing aboard, tall wooden poles with wires along the road. In the center foreground a Korean boy about ten years old in a white cotton jeogori and baggy trousers with a long braid down his back grips his father's hand and grins up at the tram, one foot on the step. The lower third of the canvas stays quiet and uncluttered so a dialogue panel can sit there, and the top sixth stays plain. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting across the whole album.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level medium-wide view, 35mm-like field, the main figures at mid distance slightly left of center, background softens into pale wash.
# 3. Lighting
Clear morning sunlight from the upper right, long soft shadows, bright but gentle.
# 4. Color grading
Sepia watercolor palette: paper cream #EFE4CF, sepia wash #C9A574, deep ink brown #3B2A1E, muted indigo #5B6E8C, faded rust #9A4A3B. Around 3800K, low saturation.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish.

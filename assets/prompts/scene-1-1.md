# 1. Scene
An 1880s Korean market day seen as a hand-painted watercolor memory: a dirt market lane lined with straw-mat stalls of pottery, grain sacks and brass ware, a merchant holding up a set of brass spoons. In the center foreground a Korean boy about ten years old in a white cotton jeogori and baggy trousers with a long braid down his back looks up at two grown men in white clothes and black horsehair hats who lean toward each other whispering, one frowning and one curious. A low thatched roof line and a far hill close the scene. The lower third of the canvas stays quiet and uncluttered so a dialogue panel can sit there, and the top sixth stays plain. Hand-painted watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish, the same painterly handwriting across the whole album.
# 2. Camera
Eye-level medium-wide view, 35mm-like field, the main figures at mid distance slightly left of center, background softens into pale wash.
# 3. Lighting
Overcast daylight from the upper left, soft open shadows with gentle contrast.
# 4. Color grading
Sepia watercolor palette: paper cream #EFE4CF, sepia wash #C9A574, deep ink brown #3B2A1E, muted indigo #5B6E8C, faded rust #9A4A3B. Around 3800K, low saturation.
# 5. Texture/Medium
Watercolor on cold-pressed paper with thin ink line work, visible paper grain, soft pigment blooms at edges, slightly faded like an old photograph, matte finish.
